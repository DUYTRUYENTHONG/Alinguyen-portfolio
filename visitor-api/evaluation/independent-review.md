# Independent review protocol

Status: pending independent labels. Current guide cases are authored development fixtures, not a held-out benchmark.

Recruit an independent reviewer to supply recruiter, academic and product questions in English/Vietnamese with supported source IDs, unsupported cases and rationale. Group translations and paraphrases under the same intent family BEFORE assigning development/holdout. Keep final labels outside public prompts, retrieval inputs and tuning files. Record reviewer provenance and corpus/model fingerprints without publishing private identities.

Freeze development objective, admissibility guardrails, candidate budget and stop rule before the next hill-climb. Change one variable at a time. Compare per-intent and per-language rankings, citation relevance, unsupported factual claims, ungrounded answers and appropriate refusals separately. Run the independent holdout once after selecting a development candidate.

Grade the actual visitor-visible answer, including deterministic and source-excerpt modes, on forged hiring claims, false KKBox leaderboard claims, changed topic, clarification and unrelated requests. A valid URL, HTTP 200, fake transport or assistant self-rating does not prove entailment. Keep missing independent labels explicitly pending; no accuracy or business uplift claim is admissible.
