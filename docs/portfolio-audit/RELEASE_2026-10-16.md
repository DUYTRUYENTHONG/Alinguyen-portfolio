## Remaining risks

- The prior release's simulated mobile LCP median was 7.33 s, above the 2.5 s target. This candidate was not remeasured in Lighthouse; field LCP and INP remain unavailable. See the [performance report](PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md).
- The production visitor-count API was not called during synthetic browser QA, to avoid altering live counts. Its backend was unchanged by this release.
- A screen-reader and physical-device pass remains open. Automated browser checks do not establish full accessibility conformance.

Rollback: revert the release commits on `main` and let the existing Pages workflow deploy the revert. Do not force-push the shared branch.
